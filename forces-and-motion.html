GRADE 7 · PHYSICS

Forces and Motion

What makes an object speed up, slow down or stay still? Experiment with applied force, mass and friction. Use the model to investigate Newton's second law and explain your evidence.

1. Learning objectives

  • Describe how a force can change an object's motion.
  • Explain the relationship between net force, mass and acceleration.
  • Investigate how friction affects motion.
  • Use measurements from a model to support a conclusion.

Key science idea

Newton's second law states that acceleration depends on net force and mass:

a = Fnet ÷ m

Force is measured in newtons (N), mass in kilograms (kg), and acceleration in metres per second squared (m/s²).

2. Predict before testing

Write a prediction. What do you think will happen if you increase the applied force while keeping mass the same?

A good prediction names the variable you will change and the result you expect.
