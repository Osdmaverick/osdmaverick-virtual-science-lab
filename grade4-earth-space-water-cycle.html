Water Cycle and Weather Patterns

Trace the Sun-powered movement of water and distinguish weather observations from climate patterns.

Learning objective

Describe the continuous movement of water above and on Earth's surface and explain the Sun's role; differentiate weather from climate (Grade 4 TEKS 4.10(A), 4.10(C)).

Prediction

Explore the model

Scientific idea

The Sun supplies energy that drives evaporation. Water vapour cools and condenses into clouds, then returns as precipitation and moves across or into the ground. Weather describes short-term conditions; climate describes long-term patterns.
