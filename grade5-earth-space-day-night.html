Earth's Rotation, Day and Night

Explore how Earth's rotation creates the daily pattern of sunlight and changing shadows.

Learning objective

Demonstrate that Earth rotates once approximately every 24 hours and explain the day/night cycle and apparent movement of the Sun, including changes in shadow position and shape (Grade 5 TEKS 5.9).

Scientific idea

Earth rotates from west to east. This makes the Sun appear to move across the sky from east to west. Shadows point away from the light source and change direction and length during the day.
