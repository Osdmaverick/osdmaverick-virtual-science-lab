Identify Earth's spheres and explore how rocks form and change through the rock cycle.

Learning objective

Scientific idea

Earth's spheres interact. The geosphere includes rocks and solid Earth; the hydrosphere includes water; the atmosphere is the envelope of gases; and the biosphere includes living things. Rocks form through different geologic processes.
