🛞 Friction and Net Force

Compare applied force and friction to determine net force and explain how balanced or unbalanced forces affect motion.

Learning objective

Compare applied force and friction to determine net force and explain how balanced or unbalanced forces affect motion. TEKS alignment and model limits: Connects to Grade 7 TEKS 7.7(D), which asks students to analyze balanced and unbalanced forces and the state of motion using Newton's First Law. Balanced forces leave an object at rest or moving at constant velocity; unbalanced forces change its velocity. This one-dimensional comparison assumes friction acts opposite the applied force and omits normal force, static/kinetic transitions, and free-body diagrams. The calculator does not simulate velocity or terminal motion.

Scientific idea

Friction opposes relative motion. With zero net force, an object remains at rest or continues at constant velocity; a non-zero net force changes its velocity in the direction of the net force.
