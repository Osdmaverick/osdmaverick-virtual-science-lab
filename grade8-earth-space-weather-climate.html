Weather, Ocean Systems and Climate

Explore how solar energy, ocean temperature, atmospheric circulation and moisture interact to influence weather and tropical cyclones.

Learning objective

Describe how energy from the Sun, hydrosphere, and atmosphere interact to influence weather and climate; identify global atmospheric movement patterns; and describe ocean-current and air-mass interactions that produce tropical cyclones (Grade 8 TEKS 8.10(A)–(C)).
