GRADE 9 · BIOLOGY

Cell Structure and Function

Explore the structures inside plant and animal cells. Select organelles, investigate their functions and compare how eukaryotic cells are organized to keep organisms alive.

1. Learning objectives

  • Identify major structures in plant and animal cells.
  • Explain the functions of the nucleus, cell membrane, cytoplasm, mitochondria and other organelles.
  • Compare plant and animal cell structures.
  • Relate organelle structure to its function.
  • Use evidence from the cell model to explain how cells carry out life processes.

Key concept

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. Eukaryotic cells contain a nucleus and specialized membrane-bound organelles.

2. Investigation question

How do specialized structures help a cell perform its functions?

4. Compare plant and animal cells

🌿 Plant cells

  • Cellulose cell wall outside the cell membrane.
  • Chloroplasts in photosynthetic plant cells.
  • Usually a large central vacuole.
  • Also contain a nucleus, cytoplasm and mitochondria.

🐾 Animal cells

  • No cellulose cell wall.
  • No chloroplasts.
  • Usually have smaller vacuoles, if present.
  • Contain a nucleus, cytoplasm and mitochondria.

Cell structures vary among cell types. Not every plant cell contains chloroplasts; for example, most root cells do not.
