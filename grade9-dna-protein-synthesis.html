GRADE 9 · BIOLOGY

DNA and Protein Synthesis

DNA → RNA → Protein

Investigate DNA base pairing, transcription and translation to discover how genetic information guides protein production.

Learning objective

Model how a DNA coding sequence is transcribed into mRNA and translated into an amino-acid chain.

1. Enter a DNA coding strand

Enter a sequence using A, T, C and G. Use a multiple of three bases (maximum 30).

This tool treats the entered sequence as the DNA coding strand (5′ to 3′). The mRNA sequence matches it except T is replaced with U.

2. The central dogma

  1. DNA replication: DNA is copied before cell division.
  2. Transcription: a gene sequence is used to make RNA.
  3. Translation: ribosomes read mRNA codons to assemble amino acids.

Three RNA bases form a codon. AUG is a start codon; UAA, UAG and UGA are stop codons.
