Natural Selection Experiment

Run a virtual population experiment to investigate how inherited variation and environmental pressures change trait frequencies over generations.

Learning objective

Imagine a population with two inherited colour forms. Better-camouflaged individuals are less likely to be eaten and may leave more offspring.
