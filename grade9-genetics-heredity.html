Genetics and Heredity

Punnett Square Simulator

Explore dominant and recessive alleles, predict offspring genotypes and phenotypes, and calculate inheritance probabilities.

Learning objective

Use a Punnett square to predict possible offspring outcomes from parental alleles.

1. Set up a genetic cross

In this model, A is a dominant allele and a is a recessive allele. Choose one allele pair for each parent.

This is a simplified single-gene model with complete dominance; real inheritance can be more complex.

2. Understand the terms

Genotype means the allele combination (AA, Aa or aa). Phenotype means the observable characteristic.

Homozygous: two identical alleles. Heterozygous: two different alleles. A dominant allele can mask a recessive allele in a heterozygote.
