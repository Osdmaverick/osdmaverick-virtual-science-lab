Homeostasis

Negative Feedback and Body Temperature

Model how negative feedback helps keep internal conditions stable when the surrounding environment changes.

Learning objective

Explain how receptors, control centres and effectors work together in a negative feedback loop.

1. Change the environment

2. Feedback response

Apply conditions to see the body's response.

The model is conceptual. It does not predict an individual's actual core temperature or clinical response.

3. Negative feedback pathway

Stimulus → Receptors → Control centre (hypothalamus) → Effectors → Response reduces the original change

If the body gets too warm

Sweat glands produce sweat; skin blood vessels dilate, increasing heat loss.

If the body gets too cool

Muscles may shiver; skin blood vessels constrict, reducing heat loss.
