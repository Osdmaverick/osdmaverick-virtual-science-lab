GRADE 9 · BIOLOGY

Human Body Systems

Integrated Systems Investigation

Investigate how the circulatory, respiratory, digestive, nervous and muscular systems coordinate to maintain body function during activity.

Learning objective

Describe how multiple organ systems interact to deliver oxygen and nutrients and remove wastes.

1. Choose an activity

Values are illustrative teaching estimates, not personal medical measurements.

2. Explore system interactions

Select a system to learn its role.

3. Trace the pathway

  1. Air enters the lungs; oxygen diffuses into the blood at the alveoli.
  2. The heart pumps oxygenated blood through arteries to body tissues.
  3. Muscle cells use oxygen and nutrients to release energy from glucose.
  4. Carbon dioxide moves into the blood and is carried to the lungs for exhalation.
  5. The digestive system absorbs nutrients; the nervous system coordinates changes in activity.
