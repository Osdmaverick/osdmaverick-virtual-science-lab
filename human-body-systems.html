GRADE 7 · BIOLOGY · VIRTUAL INVESTIGATION

Human Body Systems

Investigate how physical activity affects the body's need to move blood and oxygen around the body.

1. Learning objectives

  • Describe the roles of the circulatory, respiratory, digestive and muscular systems.
  • Relate physical activity to changes in heart rate and breathing rate.
  • Use observations from a model to support a scientific explanation.

2. Make a prediction

What do you predict will happen to heart rate and breathing rate as activity becomes more intense?

3. Run the investigation

4. Explore the body systems

The heart pumps blood through blood vessels. Blood transports oxygen and nutrients to cells and carries some waste products away.

5. Check your understanding

1. Which organ pumps blood around the body?
2. Which system brings oxygen into the lungs?
3. What usually happens to breathing rate during vigorous activity?
4. Which system breaks food down so nutrients can be absorbed?
5. Why do working muscles need more oxygen?

6. Lab report and conclusion
