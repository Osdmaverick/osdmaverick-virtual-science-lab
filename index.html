For curious learners

Think like a scientist

Every investigation follows a simple scientific workflow: prepare, predict, investigate, record and explain.

  1. PrepareRead the question and key science ideas.
  2. PredictWrite what you think will happen.
  3. InvestigateChange a variable and observe the model.
  4. RecordKeep a clear record of your results.
  5. ExplainUse evidence to support your conclusion.
