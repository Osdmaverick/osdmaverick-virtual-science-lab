GRADE 7 · CHEMISTRY

States of Matter

Explore how particles behave in solids, liquids and gases. Add or remove thermal energy and investigate melting, freezing, evaporation, condensation and the particle model of matter.

1. Learning objectives

  • Describe the arrangement and movement of particles in solids, liquids and gases.
  • Explain how heating and cooling change particle motion.
  • Identify melting, freezing, evaporation, condensation and boiling.
  • Use observations to explain changes of state.

Key idea

Heating transfers energy to a substance. Its particles generally move faster. Cooling removes thermal energy, so particle movement generally slows.

2. Predict before testing

What do you think will happen to the particles when a solid is heated?

Think about particle arrangement, movement and the energy transferred.

4. Changes of state

Heating

  • Melting: solid → liquid
  • Evaporation / boiling: liquid → gas

Cooling

  • Condensation: gas → liquid
  • Freezing: liquid → solid

For the simplified water model at ordinary atmospheric pressure, melting/freezing occurs around 0 °C and boiling/condensation around 100 °C. Real substances have different melting and boiling points.
